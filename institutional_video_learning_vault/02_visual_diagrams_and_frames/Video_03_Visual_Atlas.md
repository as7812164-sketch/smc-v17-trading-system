### Executive Visual Forensic Summary
Direct visual extraction from Episode 03 (`https://youtu.be/FtJ79wQgpOA`) demonstrates Shoaib Khan's exact gap classification and visual boundaries:
- **Primary Asset:** Forex / Gold chart (`1.3700` to `1.3780` price band).
- **Gap Highlighting:** Purple rectangular boxes drawn between Candle 1's wick and Candle 3's wick.
- **5 Gap Taxonomy Visually Demonstrated:**
  1. **Fair Value Gap (FVG):** Clean 3-candle imbalance with zero wick overlap.
  2. **Balanced Price Range (BPR):** Dual overlapping bullish and bearish FVGs creating a high-velocity pivot.
  3. **Volume Imbalance (VI):** Wicks overlap, but candle bodies leave a void between Open and Close.
  4. **Gap Imbalance / Vacuum Imbalance:** True price gap without traded volume between sessions.
  5. **Breakaway Gap:** Explosive momentum gap that leaves POIs unmitigated during major expansion.
