### Part 3: What is a Mitigation Block & Where Does It Form? (06:40 – 09:35)
- **In a Bullish Trend:**
  1. Price makes a swing high before initiating a pullback leg.
  2. Price pushes up, breaks above this swing high, and **CLOSES WITH A FULL BODY ABOVE IT**.
  3. Identify the specific candle on the previous swing high that printed the **ABSOLUTE HIGHEST HIGH**.
  4. **Color Rule:** Same as Order Block — **COLOR DOES NOT MATTER!** Green or Red, only the highest high matters.
  5. Mark that entire candle. When price pulls back, it taps this candle (the previous broken high's extreme candle) and rallies.
  6. **This candle is the Bullish Mitigation Block!**
- **In a Bearish Trend:**
  1. Price makes a swing low before a pullback.
  2. Price breaks below this swing low and closes with a full body below it.
  3. Identify the specific candle on that swing low that printed the **ABSOLUTE LOWEST LOW** (color doesn't matter).
  4. When price retraces up, it taps this candle and crashes downward.
  5. **This candle is the Bearish Mitigation Block!**

What separates a high-probability Mitigation Block from one that fails?
- **The Real-Life Wall Analogy (1-Brick vs 2-Brick Wall):**

### Part 7: How to Refine a Mitigation Block for Sniper Entries (22:05 – 25:05)
- **The Refinement Problem:** Often, the extreme candle on the broken swing is very tall, resulting in an undesirably large Stop Loss.
- **The Wick Refinement Technique:**
  - In an Uptrend: Examine the **TOP WICK** of the mitigation candle.
  - If an open FVG overlaps specifically with the **TOP WICK** of the mitigation candle:
  - **INSTEAD OF MARKING THE ENTIRE CANDLE, MARK ONLY THE TOP WICK!**
  - This shrinks the zone from a large block to a razor-sharp sniper level!
  - In a Downtrend: If an FVG overlaps with the **BOTTOM WICK**, mark only the bottom wick!
- **Gold Chart Demonstration:** Shoaib Khan shows real Gold chart evidence where only the wick was marked. Price came into the wick, and not a single candle could close below the refined wick level! Instant multi-R reversal with minimal drawdown.
- **Beginner Rule:** Beginners should mark the full candle until they master wick refinement.

### Part 8: Definition of Breaker Block & The Fundamental Difference from Mitigation (25:05 – 27:00)
Both blocks appear at broken swing extremes, but their structural origin is completely opposite:
- **Mitigation Block:**
  - Price forms Swing High, pulls back, breaks above Swing High, and taps the High's candle.
  - **CRUCIAL:** Price **DID NOT sweep or break below the previous pullback low** before breaking the High!
  - It strictly CONTINUES the existing trend.
- **Breaker Block:**
  - Market is in a downtrend.
  - Price prints a Swing Low, retraces to create a Swing High.
  - Price **FIRST sweeps / breaks below the Swing Low**!
  - Then price violently reverses and **breaks / body closes above the Swing High**!
  - **Both sides of liquidity were purged (Low swept, then High broken)!**
  - The extreme candle of that broken Swing High is the **BREAKER BLOCK**!
