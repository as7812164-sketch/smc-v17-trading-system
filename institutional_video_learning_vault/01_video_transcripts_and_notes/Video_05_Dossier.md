### 3. What is a Mitigation Block & How to Identify It? (06:40 – 09:35)
- **Bullish Mitigation Block:**
  1. Price prints a Swing High (Point A) and pulls back.
  2. Price breaks above Point A with a **FULL BODY CLOSE**.
  3. Look at Point A: Locate the candle that printed the **ABSOLUTE HIGHEST HIGH** of that swing.
  4. **Color Rule:** Same as Ep 04 — **COLOR DOES NOT MATTER!** Only the highest high candle matters.
  5. Mark this candle. Price retraces, taps this candle, and launches into continuation.
  6. **Crucial Condition:** Price **DID NOT sweep or break the previous pullback low** before breaking Point A!
- **Bearish Mitigation Block:**
  1. Price prints a Swing Low and bounces.
  2. Price breaks below the Swing Low with a **FULL BODY CLOSE**.
  3. Locate the candle that printed the **ABSOLUTE LOWEST LOW** (color doesn't matter).
  4. Retracement taps this candle and dumps.
