When price breaks into All-Time Highs (e.g. Gold at $3,500+ or BTC at $70,000+), there is no historical data on the left.

### 1. Target Setting at ATH: Institutional Round Numbers
- Target psychological round numbers:
  - Gold: `$3,500 \longrightarrow $3,510 \longrightarrow $3,520 \longrightarrow $3,550`
  - Bitcoin: `$65,000 \longrightarrow $70,000 \longrightarrow $75,000 \longrightarrow $80,000`

### 2. Top / Reversal Detection: "The 3-Candle ATH Reversal Top"
- **Candle 1:** Impulsive bullish candle expanding into new ATH.
- **Candle 2:** Sweeps Candle 1's high with an upper wick, but **closes its candle body back BELOW Candle 1's high** (Liquidity sweep & rejection).
- **Candle 3:** Closes further down with a clean bearish body below Candle 2.
- **Confirmation:** The 3-Candle Top confirms institutional exhaustion. Bias shifts DOWNSIDE targeting the nearest discount POI below!
