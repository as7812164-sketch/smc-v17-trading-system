### 7. Trading Psychology & Risk-to-Reward (R:R) Dynamics (16:30 — 19:30)
- **What is Risk-to-Reward (R:R)?**
  - How many dollars you risk on your Stop Loss vs how many dollars you target on your Take Profit.
- **Deconstruction of Standard R:R Ratios:**
  - **1:0.5 (Risk $10 to make $5):** Absolute retail madness; guarantees long-term ruin.
  - **1:1 (Risk $10 to make $10):** A fatal illusion. Due to broker spreads, slippage, and overnight financing/commissions, a 50% win rate on 1:1 will steadily drain your capital.
  - **1:2 (Risk $10 to make $20):** Acceptable, but provides minimal safety cushion during drawdown streaks.
  - **1:3 (Risk $10 to make $30):** **THE INSTITUTIONAL SWEET SPOT!**
    - High enough to yield rapid compounding.
    - Realistic enough that the market hits the target consistently without turning around.
  - **1:5, 1:10+ (Macro Moonshots):** Excellent when riding Daily HTF bias, but lower frequency.
- **Shoaib Khan's Dynamic Breakeven Protocol:**
  - When a trade reaches **1:1 or 1:1.5 in profit**:
  - **Move Stop Loss to Entry (Breakeven + Fee Shield)!**
  - Once at Breakeven, the trade is $0.00 risk. You can hold stress-free all the way to **1:3 Take Profit** without emotional interference.
  - **Strict Mandate:** Never close a trade prematurely before 1:3 once risk is neutralized!
