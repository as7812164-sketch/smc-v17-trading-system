#### [00:20:31 — 00:24:30] Finding Bias at All-Time Highs (ATH) & 3-Candle Reversal Pattern
- **The ATH Problem:** When an asset (like Gold or Bitcoin) is trading at All-Time Highs, there is no historical price action or POI on the left side of the chart.
- **Solution 1: Psychological Round Numbers:**
  - Targets are set using institutional round numbers (e.g., $3,500, $3,510, $3,520).
- **Solution 2: The 3-Candle Exhaustion Top Pattern:**
  - **Candle 1:** Strong impulsive bullish candle establishing a new high.
  - **Candle 2:** Wicks above Candle 1's high (purging buy-stop liquidity), but fails to sustain and closes back down (rejection wick).
  - **Candle 3:** Bearish candle closing below Candle 2's low, confirming the swing top.
  - When this 3-candle pattern prints at ATH on Weekly/Daily, it signals an institutional reversal or deep pullback toward discount POIs!
