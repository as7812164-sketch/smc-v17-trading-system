## 🧱 MODULE 2: THE 7 POIs TAXONOMY & STRICT INSTITUTIONAL PRIORITY STACK

From Frame 01 & Frame 02 of Video 16 (`[00:05:50 — 00:08:50]`), Shoaib Khan classifies institutional points of interest into two categories:

### 1. Blocks (5 Types)
1. **OB (Order Block):** The last opposing candle before aggressive displacement that sweeps liquidity and leaves an FVG.
2. **RB (Rejection Block):** The long wick formed at a swing extreme where price swept liquidity and aggressively closed back inside. Trapped volume acts as a refined POI.
3. **ROB (Reclaimed Order Block):** A previously mitigated order block that price temporarily breached and reclaimed with a strong body close.
4. **MB (Mitigation Block):** An order block that failed without sweeping liquidity; weaker than a breaker block.
5. **BB (Breaker Block):** An order block that successfully swept liquidity before failing. Slicing through it flips its polarity (Resistance $\leftrightarrow$ Support).

### 2. Gaps (2 Types)
1. **FVG (Fair Value Gap):** 3-candle imbalance where Candle 1 wick and Candle 3 wick leave an unfilled void.
2. **Imbalance / Volume Imbalance:** Aggressive market order injection leaving single-sided order volume or unclosed wick gaps.

### 3. The Strict Hierarchy Priority Equation (Frame 02)
When multiple POIs cluster together, institutional algorithms prioritize them in this exact order:
$$\mathbf{Order\ Block\ (OB)} > \mathbf{Imbalance} > \mathbf{Fair\ Value\ Gap\ (FVG)}$$
