## 🏛️ PART 1: CORE MARKET MECHANICS — THE CHOR-POLICE & FOOTBALL ANALOGIES

### 1. The Chor-Police Delivery Loop (`[00:01:14 — 00:02:39]`)
- Price does NOT move randomly; it plays an endless loop between **two Key Levels**: **POI (Point of Interest)** and **Liquidity**:
  $$\text{POI} \longrightarrow \text{Liquidity} \longrightarrow \text{POI} \longrightarrow \text{Liquidity} \dots$$
- If price is currently at a **POI**, its next destination is **Liquidity**.
- Once price sweeps/hunts **Liquidity**, its next destination shifts toward a **POI**.
- **Goal of Bias:** Identify the exact current origin (POI or Liquidity) and the nearest opposing target so every lower-timeframe entry aligns with the dominant institutional flow and minimizes Stop Loss hits.

### 2. The Football Pitch Analogy (`[00:02:40 — 00:04:40]`)
- **Football** = Price | **Players** = Key Levels (POIs & Liquidity) | **Goal** = Final External Liquidity Objective.
- Each Key Level holds different institutional strength ("force" behind the kick). Strong POIs propel price across massive distances; weak intermediate levels only give minor bounces toward the main target.

## 💧 PART 3: THE KEY LIQUIDITY CATEGORIES (`[00:07:04 — 00:10:30]`)

1. **Low Resistance Liquidity (LRL) vs High Resistance Liquidity (HRL):**
   - **High Resistance Liquidity (HRL):** Price leg cluttered with multiple unmitigated FVGs, Order Blocks, and structural swings. Price moves slowly, bouncing off intermediate levels step by step.
   - **Low Resistance Liquidity (LRL):** Clean, impulsive leg containing virtually ZERO opposing FVGs or Order Blocks. Price slices straight through LRL toward the target at maximum velocity.
2. **Time-Anchored Candle Liquidity (`M / W / D Liquidity`):**
   - Previous **Monthly** High & Low (note: inside-bar monthly/weekly candles require marking BOTH the inside bar and mother candle highs/lows!).
   - Previous **Weekly** High & Low.
   - Previous **Daily** High & Low.
   - *Strict Rule:* NEVER mark liquidity on the **currently open / running candle**; mark only completed historical candles.
3. **Swing High / Swing Low Liquidity:**
   - Structural swing highs (BSL) and swing lows (SSL), including Equal Highs (`EQH`) and Equal Lows (`EQL`).

## 🔄 PART 4: THE 4-STEP REVERSAL SEQUENCE ("CAR U-TURN" RULE) (`[00:19:39 — 00:25:45]`)

When multiple POIs sit stacked near price (e.g., 2 FVGs + 1 OB), **never guess blindly which POI will hold**. Like a car taking a U-turn rather than reversing instantly in reverse gear, price must complete **4 mandatory steps** (confirmed visually in Frame 04):

1. **Step 1 — Tapping:** Candle taps into the candidate POI zone. *(Do NOT enter blindly here!)*
2. **Step 2 — Displacement (`*MSS`):** Price launches impulsively away from the POI, executing a Market Structure Shift (`*MSS`) on the lower timeframe and leaving unmitigated LTF POIs inside the displacement leg. *(This proves the POI has real institutional power; un-respected POIs above/below become obsolete.)*
3. **Step 3 — Retest:** The next candle pulls back to fill/tap the **last LTF POI** inside the Step 2 displacement candle. *(Primary Sniper Entry Trigger!)*
4. **Step 4 — Expansion:** Price expands violently toward the nearest opposing Liquidity Target.
