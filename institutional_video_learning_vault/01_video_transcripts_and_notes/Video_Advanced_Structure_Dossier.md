### 2. The 3-Candle Fractal: STH & STL Mechanics (06:00 – 14:00)
- **Short Term Low (STL):**
  - Requires 3 consecutive candles.
  - Candle 1 (Left): Low is higher than Candle 2.
  - Candle 2 (Center): Lowest low of the 3-candle sequence.
  - Candle 3 (Right): Low is higher than Candle 2.
  - *Mathematical Formula:* $\text{Low}_2 < \text{Low}_1 \land \text{Low}_2 < \text{Low}_3$.
- **Short Term High (STH):**
  - Candle 1 (Left): High is lower than Candle 2.
  - Candle 2 (Center): Highest high of the 3-candle sequence.
  - Candle 3 (Right): High is lower than Candle 2.
  - *Mathematical Formula:* $\text{High}_2 > \text{High}_1 \land \text{High}_2 > \text{High}_3$.
