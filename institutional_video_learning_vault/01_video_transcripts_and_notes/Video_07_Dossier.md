### 2. Low Resistance Liquidity (LRL) vs High Resistance Liquidity (HRL) (02:00 – 04:00)
- **Etymology of "Resistance":**
  - Shoaib Khan explains the physics origin: **Resistance = "Rukawat" (Friction / Obstacle).**
  - In trading, resistance represents an obstacle that restricts price movement.
- **High Resistance Liquidity (HRL):**
  - Zones constructed by **Big Players / Institutions** (Active POIs, Order Blocks, FVGs, Mitigation Blocks, Breaker Blocks).
  - Price CANNOT easily slice through HRL.
  - Price is forced to **consolidate, fight, stall, and generate additional liquidity/fuel** before breaking or reversing.
- **Low Resistance Liquidity (LRL):**
  - Zones constructed by **Retail Traders** (Retail stop losses, lack of institutional defense).
  - Price encounters virtually ZERO friction.
  - Price travels through LRL at **supersonic displacement speed (Fast Momentum Moves)!**

### 5. External Range Liquidity (ERL) vs Internal Range Liquidity (IRL) (15:30 – 20:00)
- **Anatomy of the Structure:**
  - In any swing leg between a major Higher Low and Higher High:
    - **External Liquidity (ERL):** Resting above the Higher High (Buy Stops) and resting below the Higher Low (Sell Stops).
    - **Internal Liquidity (IRL):** Resting inside the range between the extremes (minor pullback swings / IDM, internal FVGs, internal Order Blocks).
- **The Perpetual Institutional Delivery Law:**
  $$\mathbf{ERL \longleftrightarrow IRL}$$
  1. Market sweeps or breaks External Liquidity (BOS / Swing High sweep).
  2. Institutional fuel at the high is consumed.
  3. Market MUST now reverse inward to harvest **Internal Liquidity** (pullback to internal FVG / Order Block / IDM).
  4. Once Internal Liquidity is filled and absorbed at the POI, the market MUST reverse outward to target the next **External Liquidity** pool (the new swing high)!

### 6. Candle-Based Liquidity & The Daily Bias Predictor (20:00 – 26:20)
- Highs and Lows of higher timeframe candles (Daily, Weekly, Monthly) represent immense institutional liquidity pools (PDH, PDL, PWH, PWL, PMH, PML).
- **The 2 Universal Daily Bias Outcomes:**
  - **Outcome A: Liquidity Sweep (Reversal Engine):**
    - Current candle pierces Previous Day High with a wick, but **closes back inside / below the PDH**!
    - *Classification:* **Liquidity Sweep / Hunt / Grab**.
    - *Next Day Bias:* **Bearish Expansion Day!** (Big players swept retail buy stops and sold into the market; next candle expands downward).
    - If candle pierces Previous Day Low with a wick and closes above PDL $\implies$ **Bullish Expansion Day!**
  - **Outcome B: Liquidity Run (Continuation Engine):**
    - Current candle breaks through Previous Day High and **full-body closes above PDH**!
    - *Classification:* **Liquidity Run (Expansion)**.
    - *Next Day Bias:* **Bullish Continuation Day!** (Market has sufficient institutional momentum to keep running; next candle expands higher).
- *Multi-Timeframe Fractal Law:* This rule applies identically to Weekly candles (predicting the next week's direction) and Monthly candles.
