### Pillar 1: Valid Pullback Identification (Episode 01)
- **The Core Law:** Market structure is completely invalid if based on clumsy candle wicks. A valid pullback requires the **previous candle's extreme (High/Low) to be swept**.
- **Inside Bar Filtration:** Inside bars (`high <= high[1]` and `low >= low[1]`) are **mathematical noise**. They cannot create pullbacks.
- **Single-Candle Pullback:** In aggressive momentum legs, a single candle sweeping the prior candle's high/low creates a valid pullback.

### Pillar 2: High/Low Structural Mapping & Break of Structure (Episode 02)
- **BOS (Break of Structure):** Continuation of trend. Requires **CANDLE BODY CLOSE** beyond the confirmed swing high/low. Wick piercing is NOT a BOS—it is a liquidity sweep!
- **CHoCH (Change of Character):** First sign of trend reversal. Occurs when price breaks the internal swing high/low that led to the last confirmed swing extreme.
- **Swing Confirmation:** A swing high (in bullish trend) is ONLY confirmed once price sweeps the first valid pullback (Inducement).

### Pillar 3: True Inducement (IDM) & Trap Architecture (Episode 03)
