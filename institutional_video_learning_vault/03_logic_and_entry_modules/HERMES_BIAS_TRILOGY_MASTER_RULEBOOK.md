## 1. CORE LOGIC (Market Mechanics)
1. **Perpetual Delivery Loop (`Chor-Police` / `Lion-Cave-Deer`):**
   - Market moves strictly between two Key Levels: **POI (Point of Interest)** and **Liquidity** (`POI -> Liquidity -> POI -> Liquidity`).
   - **Main Bias (`POI -> Liquidity`):** Price leaves a POI to hunt an opposing Liquidity Pool.
   - **Bias Complete Law:** Once external Liquidity is hunted, that directional bias objective is 100% finished. Stop forcing trend continuation; prepare for **Reversal (`Liquidity -> POI`)** or wait for a fresh swing above the swept liquidity.
2. **50/50 Equal Weight Law (Ep 17):**
   - Marking only POIs while ignoring Liquidity leaves you blind on targets and exits. **50% weight = POI | 50% weight = Liquidity.**
3. **Maze Zoom-Out & HTF Dominance (`Heavy Truck vs Maruti Alto`):**
   - Lower timeframes (`5m/15m`) are inside the labyrinth. Bias MUST be derived top-down from **`1M (Monthly) -> 1W (Weekly) -> 1D (Daily)`**.
   - Weekly/Daily (`Heavy Truck`) dominates `4H/1H` (`Maruti Alto`). After a `1M/1W/1D` liquidity sweep, opposing `1H/4H` POIs lack institutional power to reverse price until a `1D/1W` POI is reached.

## 2. EXACT RULES (Marking, Regimes & Confirmation)

   - **5 Blocks:** `OB` (Order Block), `RB` (Rejection Block), `ROB` (Reclaimed OB), `MB` (Mitigation Block), `BB` (Breaker Block).
   - **2 Gaps:** `FVG` (Fair Value Gap), `Imbalance / Volume Imbalance`.
   - **Strict Priority:** **`OB > Imbalance > FVG`**.
2. **Liquidity Marking Laws (Ep 16):**
   - **`M / W / D` Candle Liquidity:** Mark ONLY **completed/closed** Monthly, Weekly, and Daily candle Highs/Lows (**never mark the currently running active candle**). If the previous closed candle is an **Inside Bar**, mark BOTH the **Inside Bar AND Mother Candle** Highs/Lows!
   - **Swing Liquidity (`BSL / SSL`):** Clean unbroken swing highs and swing lows (`$$$`).
   - **LRL vs HRL:** Clean void legs (`Low Resistance Liquidity`) deliver rapidly; crowded FVG/OB legs (`High Resistance Liquidity`) stall and bounce step-by-step.
3. **Nearest Key Level Gravity Law (Ep 16 & Ep 17):**
   - Price ALWAYS executes the **nearest Key Level first** (e.g., `650 pips` away) rather than jumping straight to distant `3,000–7,000 pip` POIs.
4. **The 4 Market Movement Regimes (Ep 17 Frame 01):**
   - **1. `POI -> Liquidity`:** Main Trend Bias (**HIGHEST PROBABILITY — Trade THIS!**)
   - **2. `Liquidity -> POI`:** Counter / Reversal pullback (Risky; avoid for beginners).
   - **3. `POI -> POI`:** Large-range sideways consolidation.
   - **4. `Liquidity -> Liquidity`:** Tight choppy consolidation (**STRICTLY AVOID** — sweeps BSL and SSL repeatedly).
5. **The 4-Step Swing Reversal Rule (Ep 16 Frame 04 — NO BLIND TOUCH!):**
   - Never guess across stacked POIs; require 4 steps:
     1. **`Tapping`:** Candle 1 taps into the POI *(wait, zero entry)*.
     2. **`Displacement (*MSS)`:** Candle 2 launches away with strong impulse, clean **FVG**, and LTF structure shift (`*MSS`). *(Any POI without Step 2 displacement is invalidated.)*
     3. **`Retest`:** Candle 3 pulls back to fill the **last unmitigated LTF POI** inside Step 2's displacement leg *(Entry Trigger!)*.
     4. **`Expansion`:** Candle 4 accelerates into the nearest target Liquidity Pool.
   - **Distant MSS Exception (Ep 13):** If structural MSS is too far away, enter on retest of the clean **Displacement FVG** emerging from the POI.
6. **IDM vs MSS & Post-IDM First-POI Law (Ep 16 & Ep 13):**
   - **IDM:** First internal pullback sweep inside an HTF trend = **Continuation** (often launching straight from the very **first POI / FVG** after IDM).
   - **MSS:** Swing break at an External Liquidity Pool or HTF POI = **Reversal**.
7. **Mirror Concept (Ep 17 Frames 06–07):**
