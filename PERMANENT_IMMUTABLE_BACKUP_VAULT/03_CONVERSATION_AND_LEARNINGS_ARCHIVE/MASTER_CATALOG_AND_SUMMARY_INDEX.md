## 📚 3. 15-VIDEO INSTITUTIONAL CURRICULUM SYNTHESIS (MS TRADING / SHOAIB KHAN)

### 🔹 Episode 01: Core Market Structure & Pure Swing Points
- Valid swing high requires lower highs on both sides; valid swing low requires higher lows.
- Inside bars contained within a mother candle are completely ignored.

### 🔹 Episode 02: Valid vs. Invalid Pullbacks
- A pullback is ONLY valid if the previous candle's extreme (low in uptrend, high in downtrend) is physically breached and taken out.
- Mother-candle containment is NOT a pullback.

### 🔹 Episode 03: Inducement (IDM) & True BOS Confirmation
- Market CANNOT confirm a true Break of Structure (BOS) until the **First Internal Pullback (Inducement / IDM)** is completely swept.
- Breakouts without IDM purges are institutional traps.

### 🔹 Episode 04: Real vs. Fake BOS (Wick Sweeps)
- True BOS requires a **CANDLE BODY CLOSE** beyond the swing extreme.
- A wick breach with body close inside is a **Liquidity Sweep (Turtle Soup)** priming an aggressive counter-reversal.

### 🔹 Episode 05: Order Block Definition & Invalidation
- Definition: The last opposing delivery candle before impulsive displacement creating an FVG.
- **Shoaib Khan Invalidation Law:** An OB remains valid if price only wicks through it. The INSTANT a candle **BODY CLOSES beyond the OB boundary**, the zone is dead.

### 🔹 Episode 06: Imbalance & True Fair Value Gap (FVG)
- Geometry: Gap between Candle 1 Wick and Candle 3 Wick across a 3-candle displacement sequence.
- High-probability OBs MUST have an unmitigated FVG attached.

### 🔹 Episode 07: Single Candle Order Block (SCOB)
- If Candle 2 sweeps Candle 1 and closes in reverse within the same timeframe, Candle 2 itself becomes an independent Order Block.
